# Shadows

All three shadows are exported as `dropShadow` effects and use the brand primary teal (`#037171`) with alpha transparency, rather than pure black.

## Shadow tokens

| Token | X offset (px) | Y offset (px) | Blur / radius (px) | Spread (px) | Exported colour (RGBA hex) | Opacity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Soft Shadow | 1.5 | 6 | 30 | 0 | `#0371711f` | 12.2% |
| Medium Shadow | 4 | 6 | 24 | 0 | `#0371712e` | 18.0% |
| Hard Shadow | 6 | 10 | 20 | 0 | `#03717133` | 20.0% |

## Why use the brand colour?

A shadow built from the brand teal inherits a subtle hue from the identity, so depth feels integrated with the interface instead of appearing like a neutral black stain. A black shadow tends to read more neutral or harsh, while a translucent brand-coloured shadow can feel more cohesive; the effect still depends on the surface behind it and should be checked in context.
