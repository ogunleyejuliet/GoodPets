# Port Conflict — Recovery

**Problem:** Running `node server.js` failed with `EADDRINUSE` because port 3000 was already in use.

**Investigation:** Ran `netstat -ano | findstr :3000` and identified PID `7140` as a listening process. Ran `tasklist /FI "PID eq 7140"` and confirmed it was `node.exe`.

**Resolution:** Ran `taskkill /PID 7140 /F`. Windows confirmed that the process was terminated successfully.

**Verification:** Ran `netstat -ano | findstr :3000` again. No output appeared, confirming that nothing was listening on port 3000 at that time.
