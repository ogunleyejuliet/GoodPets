Why instant switching between authentication forms matters

Combining sign-in, sign-up, and password reset into one page allows users to switch between authentication forms smoothly without waiting for a separate page to load. This reduces friction, makes the experience feel faster, and keeps users focused on completing their task.

What the experience was like before

Before the change, authentication forms were on separate pages. Moving between them required navigating to another page, which involved a loading experience and interrupted the user's flow.

When separate pages would be a better choice

Separate pages may be a better choice when authentication flows become significantly more complex or require distinct layouts, content, or steps. Keeping those flows separate can make each page easier to understand and maintain.
