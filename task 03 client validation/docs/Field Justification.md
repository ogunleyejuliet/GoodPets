# Field Justification

## Account Registration

| Field | Why it is needed | How Good Pets uses it | Consequence of incorrect information |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email | Provides a way to contact the customer. | Sends booking notifications and other service-related communications. | The customer may not receive booking confirmations, reminders, or important updates. |
| Full name | Identifies the pet owner. | Helps staff identify the owner, particularly during pet pickup. | Staff may have difficulty confirming the correct owner and releasing the pet safely. |
| Password | Protects access to the customer's account. | Helps prevent unauthorized access to bookings and pet information if someone gains access to the customer's device. | An unauthorized person could access the account or interfere with bookings and pet-care arrangements. |

## Pet Admission Information

Customers can add multiple pets after selecting their services. Each pet profile collects the following information:

| Field | Why it is needed | How Good Pets uses it | Consequence of incorrect information |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pet name | Identifies the individual pet. | Helps staff identify the pet and associate it with the correct booking. | Staff may confuse pets or associate care instructions with the wrong animal. |
| Pet picture | Provides a visual reference for identifying the pet. | Helps staff recognize the pet during admission and care. | Staff may have difficulty visually confirming the correct pet. |
| Vaccination status | Establishes whether the pet has received the required vaccinations. | Helps Good Pets assess whether the pet meets its vaccination requirements. | Incorrect information may create health and safety risks for the pet, other animals, and staff. |
| Pet weight | Provides information about the pet's size. | Supports service planning and appropriate care. | Staff may make unsuitable care or service decisions based on inaccurate information. |
