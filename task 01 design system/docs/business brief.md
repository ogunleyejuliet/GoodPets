# GOOD PETS
## Business Brief

### 1. The Business
Good Pets is a pet-care business offering grooming, daycare, and boarding services. It helps pet owners access reliable, attentive care when they are busy, working, or travelling.

Services include:
- **Grooming:** Bathing, trimming, coat care, and nail clipping.
- **Daycare:** Supervised daytime care.
- **Boarding:** Temporary accommodation and care.
- **Recurring packages:** Options for regular grooming or daycare customers.

### 2. User Persona
**Primary users:** Pet owners aged 22–50, including working professionals, business owners, and frequent travellers.

**Devices:** Primarily smartphones, with occasional desktop or laptop use.

**Connection:** Generally reliable internet, with occasional slower mobile connections.

**Technical ability:** Generally comfortable with technology, online bookings, and digital payments. The product should remain intuitive, with clear navigation and feedback.

**Needs:** Customers want to compare services, understand prices and requirements, and arrange care without unnecessary stress.

### 3. The One Thing That Must Work
Customers must be able to find the right service for their pet, understand its requirements, check availability, and complete a booking confidently.

The product must maintain accurate booking and payment records and prevent scheduling conflicts. Pet profiles, recurring payments, customer records, and the AI assistant should support this core workflow.

### 4. Three Questions Only Good Pets Can Answer
The product's RAG-powered AI assistant should answer using Good Pets' approved business information.

1. **What grooming services and packages do you offer, and how much do they cost?** Retrieve current prices, service descriptions, and package inclusions.
2. **What does my pet need before daycare or boarding?** Retrieve the relevant documented requirements and preparation checklists.
3. **What happens if I cancel or reschedule my booking?** Retrieve the applicable cancellation, rescheduling, and refund policies.

If information is unavailable, the assistant should acknowledge this rather than invent an answer.

### 5. Interface Feeling, Colours, and Typography
**Desired feeling:** Warm, trustworthy, caring, approachable, and quietly professional.

**Brand colours:**
- **Teal:** Trust and reassurance.
- **Orange:** Energy and friendliness.
- **Beige:** Comfort and softness.
- **Brown:** Groundedness and dependability.

**Typography:** Use **Nunito Sans**, a friendly, rounded sans-serif that balances warmth with readability. Establish a clear hierarchy using expressive but restrained headings, readable body text, and medium or semibold weights for buttons and navigation. Prices, booking details, and policies must be easy to scan. Avoid decorative fonts, excessively playful lettering, and thin text weights.

The interface should be mobile-friendly, spacious, accessible, and easy to navigate, with subtle rounded elements and purposeful pet imagery. It should feel like a natural extension of a caring, dependable pet-care business—not a clinical platform or a childish app.

**Core promise:** Reliable pet care made easier for pet owners.
